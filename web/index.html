How it works

Four Cypher files do the work.

R2

Preflight

Read-only. Matches the action against fixes refuted by executed evidence.

preflight_r2.cypher
W1 · W3

Evidence gate

An approval or done claim lands only on runs that executed on that sha. Zero rows back means refused.

approve_pr.cypherclaim_done.cypher
W2

Refute

Refutes the claim and stales what relied on it, up to three hops. Never deletes.

refute_claim.cypher
